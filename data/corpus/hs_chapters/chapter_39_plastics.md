# HS Chapter 39 — Plastics and Articles Thereof

Chapter 39 is relevant to health commodity supply chains for medical plastics: IV bags, plastic syringes, tubing, packaging, and laboratory consumables.

## Key Headings Relevant to Health Commodities

| HS Heading | Description |
|---|---|
| 3923 | Articles for the conveyance or packing of goods, of plastics. Includes plastic bags, bottles, and containers used for pharmaceutical packaging. |
| 3926 | Other articles of plastics. Includes plastic laboratory consumables: pipette tips, sample cups, reagent containers. |

## Relevance to USAID Data

Plastic medical consumables (IV bags, packaging film, laboratory plasticware) appear in USAID commodity shipments as supporting supply chain materials rather than primary pharmaceuticals. These items typically:
- Have lower value-to-weight ratios than ARVs
- Are often ocean-freighted in bulk to central medical stores
- Do not require cold-chain handling
- May be sourced regionally (within Africa) rather than from global suppliers, meaning shorter freight legs

HS 39 products are sometimes bundled in shipments with Chapter 30 pharmaceuticals for the same consignee. In the USAID dataset, they may appear under generic commodity descriptions like "medical supplies" or "health commodities - other."
