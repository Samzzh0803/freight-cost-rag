# HS Chapter 30 — Pharmaceutical Products

Chapter 30 covers pharmaceutical products including medicaments, vaccines, bandages, and diagnostic reagents. This is the most relevant HS chapter for USAID health commodity shipments, covering the ARV (antiretroviral) drugs, antimalarials, and other medicines in the SCMS dataset.

## Key Headings

| HS Heading | Description |
|---|---|
| 3001 | Glands and other organs, dried; extracts of glands and organs |
| 3002 | Human blood; animal blood for therapeutic use; vaccines, toxins, cultures of micro-organisms; cell therapy |
| 3003 | Medicaments (not 3002 or 3006), for therapeutic or prophylactic use, mixed, not in dosage form |
| **3004** | **Medicaments (not 3002 or 3006), for therapeutic or prophylactic use, in measured doses (including transdermal delivery). Most ARVs and health commodities are classified here.** |
| 3005 | Wadding, gauze, bandages, and similar articles (e.g. dressings, plasters) |
| 3006 | Pharmaceutical goods: sterile surgical catgut; blood-grouping reagents; dental cements; first-aid boxes; chemical contraceptive preparations |

## Relevance to USAID SCMS Data

The majority of USAID SCMS health commodities fall under HS 3004: finished pharmaceutical preparations in dosage form. Key products:
- **ARVs (antiretrovirals):** Lopinavir/Ritonavir, Efavirenz, Tenofovir — all HS 3004
- **Antimalarials:** Artemether-Lumefantrine (Coartem) — HS 3004
- **Co-trimoxazole (prophylaxis):** HS 3004
- **Rapid diagnostic tests (RDTs):** May fall under HS 3002 (if biological) or 3822 (if chemical reagent)

## Import Duty Context

HS Chapter 30 products typically receive preferential import duty treatment in most PEPFAR recipient countries — often zero duty under bilateral trade agreements or WHO essential medicines exemptions. This affects the DDP freight cost: duty components are minimal for ARVs in most programme countries. However, VAT and excise may still apply.

## UNCTAD Coverage Note

The UNCTAD GTCDIT dataset includes commodity-group-level freight rates. HS 30 (pharmaceutical products) is one of the better-covered commodity groups in the UNCTAD data for Africa-bound trade lanes, reflecting the volume of pharmaceutical trade in the dataset period (2016–2021).
