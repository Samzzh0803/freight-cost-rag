# HS Chapter 63 — Other Made-Up Textile Articles; Sets; Worn Clothing and Worn Textile Articles; Rags

Chapter 63 is relevant to health commodity supply chains specifically for bed nets — insecticide-treated bed nets (ITNs) used for malaria prevention.

## Key Heading

| HS Heading | Description |
|---|---|
| 6304 | Other furnishing articles, excluding those of heading 9404. Includes mosquito nets and bed nets. |
| 6307 | Other made-up articles, including dress patterns. Can include medical textile products. |

## Relevance to USAID Data

Insecticide-treated bed nets (ITNs) — such as PermaNet and Olyset — are classified under HS 6304 or 6307 depending on the customs authority. They are a significant USAID/PEPFAR malaria prevention commodity. Key freight characteristics:
- **High volume, low value per unit:** Bed nets are bulky and relatively inexpensive per piece, making air freight economics poor unless urgency demands it.
- **Ocean freight preferred:** Bed net shipments are typically ocean-freighted in FCL, given the volume and the tolerance for 30–45 day transit times in malaria programme planning cycles.
- **Mode contrast:** In the USAID dataset, bed net shipments are a key reason ocean freight (mode = Ocean) appears at all — they represent the low-urgency, high-volume pole of the commodity distribution.

## Duty and Trade Notes

Bed nets receive duty-free treatment in most Sub-Saharan African countries under regional trade agreements and health product exemptions. The low duty burden means DDP and DAP freight costs are similar for bed net shipments to most PEPFAR countries.
