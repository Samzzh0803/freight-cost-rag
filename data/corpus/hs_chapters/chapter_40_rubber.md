# HS Chapter 40 — Rubber and Articles Thereof

Chapter 40 is relevant to health commodity supply chains for condoms, gloves, and other rubber-based medical products.

## Key Headings

| HS Heading | Description |
|---|---|
| 4014 | Hygienic or pharmaceutical articles of vulcanised rubber (other than hard rubber), with or without fittings of hard rubber. Includes condoms and surgical gloves. |
| 4015 | Articles of apparel and clothing accessories, of vulcanised rubber (other than hard rubber). Includes examination gloves. |

## Relevance to USAID Data

Condoms and examination gloves are significant USAID/PEPFAR commodity categories:
- **Male condoms:** Classified under HS 4014.10. A major volume commodity in the USAID dataset, representing prevention programmes.
- **Surgical/examination gloves:** HS 4015.11/19.

Key freight characteristics for condoms:
- **Very high volume, low value per unit:** Similar to bed nets — condom shipments are large-volume, price-sensitive, and unsuited to airfreight economics.
- **Ocean FCL preferred:** Condom programme replenishment is typically ocean-freighted.
- **No cold-chain requirement:** Unlike ARVs or RDTs, condoms are ambient-temperature products.
- In the USAID dataset, condom shipments contribute significantly to the small share of ocean-mode records. They represent a very different cost profile from ARV shipments: lower per-unit freight cost, higher total volume.

## Duty Notes

Condoms are classified as essential public health products and receive duty-free or reduced-duty treatment in most Sub-Saharan African countries, consistent with their status on the WHO Essential Medicines List.
