# HS Chapter 38 — Miscellaneous Chemical Products

Chapter 38 covers miscellaneous chemical products that do not fall under more specific chapters. For USAID health commodity supply chains, the most relevant heading is 3822: diagnostic reagents.

## Key Heading

| HS Heading | Description |
|---|---|
| 3822 | Diagnostic or laboratory reagents on a backing, prepared diagnostic or laboratory reagents whether or not on a backing, other than those of heading 3002 or 3006; certified reference materials. |

## Relevance to USAID Data

Rapid Diagnostic Tests (RDTs) for malaria, HIV, and other conditions are frequently classified under HS 3822 when they consist of chemical reagents on a backing. This includes:
- HIV rapid test kits (e.g. Determine HIV-1/2, OraQuick)
- Malaria RDTs (e.g. for Plasmodium falciparum detection)
- Pregnancy test kits

These products are distinct from pharmaceutical medicaments (Chapter 30) and biological products (Chapter 3002), though customs classification can vary by country.

## Freight Notes

HS 3822 products are typically high-value, low-weight, and temperature-sensitive (requiring cold chain). Airfreight with cold-chain handling (2–8°C for many RDTs) is standard. DG (dangerous goods) classification may apply to reagents containing hazardous chemicals, adding surcharges and documentation requirements. In the USAID dataset, RDT shipments appear under the "Rapid test" commodity category and are almost universally airfreighted.
