# HS Chapter 85 — Electrical Machinery, Equipment and Parts; Sound Recorders; Television Apparatus

Chapter 85 is occasionally relevant to health commodity supply chains for electronic diagnostic and monitoring equipment not classified under Chapter 90.

## Key Headings Relevant to Health Commodities

| HS Heading | Description |
|---|---|
| 8543 | Electrical machines and apparatus, having individual functions, not elsewhere specified. Can include certain point-of-care diagnostic devices. |
| 8471 | Automatic data-processing machines and units thereof. Computers and tablets used in health information systems. |

## Relevance to USAID Data

USAID PEPFAR programmes sometimes ship electronic health monitoring equipment alongside pharmaceutical commodities. These may include:
- Point-of-care CD4 testing devices with electronic readout (e.g. Pima CD4 analyser — may classify under 8543 or 9027)
- Health management information system (HMIS) hardware: tablets, laptops for data collection
- Electronic scales and temperature monitoring equipment

These items have different freight profiles from pharmaceuticals:
- Higher value-to-weight in some cases (electronic diagnostic equipment)
- No cold-chain requirement for hardware (though reagents stored separately may need cold chain)
- Potential import duty complexity — electronics duties vary widely by African country

In the USAID dataset, electronic equipment shipments are rare and typically appear as a separate line item from pharmaceutical commodity shipments.
