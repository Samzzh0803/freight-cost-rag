# HS Chapter 90 — Optical, Photographic, Medical or Surgical Instruments and Apparatus

Chapter 90 covers medical and surgical instruments and apparatus, including diagnostic equipment relevant to health commodity supply chains. Some USAID SCMS diagnostic and laboratory supply shipments fall under this chapter.

## Key Headings Relevant to Health Commodities

| HS Heading | Description |
|---|---|
| 9018 | Instruments and appliances used in medical, surgical, dental, or veterinary sciences. Includes syringes, needles, catheters, and diagnostic instruments. |
| 9019 | Mechano-therapy appliances; massage apparatus; psychological aptitude-testing apparatus; ventilators |
| 9021 | Orthopaedic appliances; splints; artificial parts of body; hearing aids; pacemakers |
| 9022 | X-ray apparatus and apparatus using alpha, beta, or gamma radiation |
| 9027 | Instruments and apparatus for physical or chemical analysis (e.g. spectrophotometers, chromatographs). Laboratory equipment for HIV diagnostic workup. |

## Relevance to USAID Data

A subset of USAID SCMS shipments includes laboratory consumables and diagnostic equipment — CD4 testing equipment, reagents, and related supplies. These may be classified under:
- HS 9018: Syringes, needles, blood collection tubes
- HS 9027: Diagnostic analysers, reagent kits for chemistry and haematology

## Freight and Duty Notes

Medical instruments under Chapter 90 generally receive more favourable duty treatment in recipient countries than general merchandise, though less universally than Chapter 30 pharmaceuticals. Some diagnostic equipment is heavy and bulky, making it unsuitable for airfreight — these shipments may appear as ocean or truck mode in the dataset. High-value equipment (e.g. CD4 analysers) has a high value-to-weight ratio and may still be airfreighted despite bulk.
